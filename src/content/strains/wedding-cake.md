Wedding Cake sits at the potent end of the dessert-hybrid category that has dominated menus for the last several years. It leans indica in effect despite the hybrid label.

The name describes the smell rather than the experience. Expect a sweet, almost bakery-like nose and an effect that is considerably heavier than that suggests.

Because it tests high, it is a strain where checking the certificate of analysis genuinely matters. Ask to see it.
