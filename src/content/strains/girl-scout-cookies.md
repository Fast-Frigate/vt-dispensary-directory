Often shortened to GSC, this one crosses OG Kush with Durban Poison and lands well above average on potency. The effect starts in the head and moves into the body fast enough that people who paced themselves on lighter flower can get caught out.

Treat the THC number on the label as a real signal here. GSC frequently tests in the mid-twenties, which is a lot if your reference point is something in the teens.

Caryophyllene leads the terpene profile, which is unusual enough to be worth noting: it is the same compound that gives black pepper its bite.
