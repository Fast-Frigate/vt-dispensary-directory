Blue Dream earned its ubiquity honestly. It crosses Blueberry with Haze, and the result leans sativa without the racy edge that sends anxious users running. The high tends to arrive gradually and settle into something functional rather than couch-locking.

For a first-time buyer walking into a Vermont dispensary, it is a reasonable default. Ask the budtender what the actual lab numbers are on the jar in front of you, because a strain name tells you far less than the test results do. Two harvests of Blue Dream from different growers can behave quite differently.

Myrcene usually dominates the terpene profile, with pinene and caryophyllene behind it. That combination reads as sweet berry with a herbal back end.
