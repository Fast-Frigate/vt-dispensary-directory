Named after the author of The Emperor Wears No Clothes, Jack Herer is the strain people recommend when someone says they want to stay functional. The effect is more clear than stimulating, which is a meaningful distinction.

Terpinolene leads the profile, which is relatively uncommon and gives it a piney, faintly sharp character that separates it from the sweeter sativas.

Worth asking about if a menu is heavy on dessert-profile hybrids and you want something different.
