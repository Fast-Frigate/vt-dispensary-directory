Sour Diesel smells like its name and does not apologise for it. The onset is quick and distinctly heady, which makes it a daytime choice for experienced users and a poor first purchase for anyone prone to anxiety.

The fuel note is genuinely strong. If you are buying to consume somewhere with neighbours nearby, factor that in.

Lineage is generally given as Chemdawg crossed with Super Skunk, though Sour Diesel predates careful record-keeping and its exact parentage is contested.
