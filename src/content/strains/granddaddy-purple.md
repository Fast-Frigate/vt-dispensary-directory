Granddaddy Purple is what most people picture when they picture an indica: dense, deeply coloured flower and an effect that makes plans for later feel optional. The purple comes from anthocyanins expressed in cool growing conditions, not from anything added.

This is a nighttime strain. Vermont dispensary staff will usually say so unprompted, but it is worth stating plainly, because the grape-candy smell reads as friendlier than the effect actually is.

Myrcene sits high in the profile, which is broadly consistent with the sedating reputation.
