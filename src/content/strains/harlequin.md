Harlequin is the answer to a question a lot of first-time buyers are actually asking: is there something that does not get me very high? Its CBD content typically runs well above its THC, often at a ratio somewhere around 2:1 or higher.

That ratio changes the experience substantially. Most people describe it as noticeable but not impairing in the way a conventional strain is.

CBD-forward flower is less common on Vermont menus than THC-dominant flower, so call ahead if this is specifically what you want. Nothing here is medical advice; if you are managing a health condition, talk to a clinician.
